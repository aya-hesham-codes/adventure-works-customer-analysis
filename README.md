Adventure Works: Customer Retention & Acquisition Analysis

A Power BI project focused on customer retention, first-purchase patterns, and customer acquisition trends.

Project Overview

This project analyzes Adventure Works customer data to understand how customers behave after their first purchase, what characteristics are associated with first purchases, and how customer acquisition may change over time.

Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Cohort Analysis
- Decomposition Tree
- Forecasting
- Data Visualization

Analysis

The dashboard contains three main areas:

- Customer Retention: Cohort analysis to measure customer retention after the first purchase.
- Customer Acquisition Drivers: Decomposition Tree to explore first purchases by region, product, gender, and customer segment.
- Customer Acquisition Forecast: 12-month forecast using a 95% confidence interval and monthly seasonality.

Key Findings

- December 2021 was the largest customer cohort with 946 new customers.
- The December 2021 cohort reached 66% retention in month 6.
- A common first-purchase path identified was Asia → Road Bikes → Female → Retail Stores.
- Customer acquisition varied significantly across months, and longer-term forecasts showed greater uncertainty.

Recommendations

- Investigate what contributed to the strong December 2021 cohort.
- Focus on improving early customer retention.
- Further analyze common first-purchase profiles.
- Consider historical seasonal patterns when using acquisition forecasts.
